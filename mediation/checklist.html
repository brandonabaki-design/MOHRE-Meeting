Mediation Checklist

Tap each item as you cover it during the call. Checkmarks persist if you close and reopen the page.

Before the call

  • Phone charger connected and do-not-disturb on for non-MOHRE contacts.Calls can run long; don't let a dead battery or notification interrupt you.
  • Water within reach.Dry mouth on a phone mediation is a real problem. Sip before you answer difficult questions.
  • Quiet room, good signal, landline backup ready.If the call drops it is not your fault — MOHRE will call back, but make sure you are reachable.
  • All exhibits open or printed: Resignation, Contract, Signed Clearance, Both EOS PDFs, Email chain.Have them indexed so you can refer to them by name.
  • Opening statement rehearsed aloud at least once.The outline page has the script.
  • Pen and paper ready to write down names, ticket numbers, and any proposal the mediator makes.

Opening points to land in the first 3 minutes

  • State my full name and that I am here in good faith to resolve this amicably.
  • Frame the dispute narrowly: a 2-day mismatch between the signed clearance and the EOS.
  • Say "I tried to do everything correctly" — and point to the paper trail.
  • Mention that I repeatedly consulted the MOHRE call centre (800 84) to make sure I was right.

Core facts I must say aloud

  • Joining date: 4 September 2025. Still inside 6-month probation when I resigned.
  • Resignation sent by email on 26 February 2026 at 1:27 PM.
  • Resignation explicitly cited Article 9(3) of FDL 33/2021 and Article 43.
  • Resignation explicitly named Saturday, 28 March 2026 as my final official day of employment.
  • The school never responded to the resignation and never objected to the 28 March date.
  • I completed handover — my role did not involve grading or classroom management.
  • Signed clearance form (31 March) records "Last Date of Employment 28-03-2026" and "30 days" of notice.HR's handwriting, Principal's signature, multiple department sign-offs.
  • Principal signed "No Balance Outstanding" on 30/03/2026.
  • First EOS (31 March) listed last day as 26 March and counted only 29 notice days total — arithmetic error.
  • Second EOS (6 April) moved last day to 27 March — still one day short of 30.
  • I cited Article 53 (14-day rule); deadline expired 11 April 2026.

Key evidence to reference by name

  • Resignation Notification email (26 Feb 2026, 1:27 PM) — addressed to the Principal, cc'd to HR and both VPs.
  • Employment Contract (Transaction No. MB298388212AE) — 2-year limited contract, 6-month probation, AED 20,000 gross.
  • Signed Clearance Form — the decisive document. Say this name at least three times during the call.
  • Clearance email sent during notice period — no objection raised.
  • First EOS — dated 31-03-2026, last day 26-03-2026, total AED 17,333.
  • Second EOS — dated 06-04-2026, last day 27-03-2026, total AED 18,000.
  • Email chain with HR and Sakha Al Dbaisi (Athena HO) showing my repeated attempts to resolve.

Things I must NOT do

  • Don't raise my voice or talk over the school's representative.
  • Don't say anything disparaging about the school, the Principal, Athena, or HR.The EOS waiver on the table has non-disparagement language; the mediator will also notice tone.
  • Don't agree to sign anything marked "full and final" or "waive right to complain" during the call.If they want the complaint withdrawn, the corrected EOS has to reach my account first.
  • Don't volunteer the verbal "no spring-break pay" conversation unless asked — but if asked, confirm it honestly and calmly.
  • Don't expand the dispute. I am not raising gratuity, leave pay, or other issues.
  • Don't speculate. If I don't know the answer, I say: "I don't recall exactly, but I can supplement the record with that."

During the call — what to write down

  • Mediator's name and title.
  • Names of everyone on the school's side.
  • Any ticket or reference number the mediator gives.
  • Any proposal the school makes — word for word.
  • Any deadline the mediator sets (e.g. "respond within 7 days").
  • Any document the mediator asks me to send — and by when.

Settlement terms I can accept

  • EOS amended to show Last Working Day: 28 March 2026.
  • March salary AED 18,666.67 (28 days).
  • Work permit cancellation processed without delay.
  • Payment transferred to my bank account before I sign the amended EOS.
  • Any waiver language in the EOS limited to the amounts correctly paid — not a blanket waiver of MOHRE rights.

If the school refuses to budge

  • Stay polite. Thank the mediator. Say I prefer to resolve today but understand if escalation is needed.
  • Ask the mediator what the next step is and what timeframe applies.
  • Confirm in writing (after the call) any commitments the mediator summarised.
  • Do not sign the existing "full and final" EOS just to make the case go away.

After the call

  • Write down everything I remember within 15 minutes of hanging up.
  • Send a polite email to the mediator summarising what was agreed.
  • File the notes, the MOHRE ticket number, and any new documents in this repository.
  • Call my brother and debrief.
