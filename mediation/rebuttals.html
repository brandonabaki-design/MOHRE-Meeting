Anticipated Objections & Measured Rebuttals

Every argument the school has already made or may make — with a calm, short response to each.

Objection 1 — "The settlement is full and final."

Sakha Al Dbaisi, 7 Apr 2026: "It's full and final as I mentioned in my email. You can proceed with MOHRE ticket."

Response: A settlement is only "full and final" if it reflects the employee's full statutory entitlement. Under Article 53, a shortfall in statutory amounts cannot be waived by a "full and final" label. The school's own signed clearance form shows 28 March and 30 days — the EOS needs to match that document, then we can close this amicably today.

Objection 2 — "February counts as 3 days (26, 27, 28)."

Sakha Al Dbaisi, 7 Apr 2026: "February salary was released on time including the three days (26, 27 and 28)."

Response: Whether February contributes 2 or 3 days of notice is not actually the issue on the table today. Even on the school's own count, the second EOS pays only 27 days in March, and the signed clearance says the last day was 28 March. The EOS dates and the clearance dates need to match. That is all I am asking for.

Objection 3 — "The clearance form is a procedural document; the EOS is what governs."

Response: The clearance form was prepared by the school's HR, filled in in HR's own handwriting, signed by the Department Supervisor, IT, Resources, Library and Operations, and then signed by the Principal on 30/03/2026 with the remark "No Balance Outstanding." It is not an informal document — it is the school's own record of my last day. If the EOS contradicts that record, the EOS is the one that needs correction, not the clearance.

Objection 4 — "You were on probation; you aren't entitled to notice pay."

Response: Probation does not mean no notice period — it means a 30-day notice when moving to another UAE employer under Article 9(3) of FDL 33/2021. I gave that notice in writing, the school accepted it without objection, the clearance form records 30 days served. My probation status is the reason the notice is 30 days and not 1–3 months — it doesn't eliminate it.

Objection 5 — "Spring break was closed; those days should not count."

Response: Article 43 of the Labour Law calculates notice periods in calendar days — weekends, public holidays, and school breaks all count. My resignation email cited Article 43 for exactly this reason. The school cannot apply Article 43 selectively.

Objection 6 — "You weren't working the last few days, you were on handover."

Response: My role was Head of Curriculum, Teaching and Learning — not classroom teaching. My resignation email stated that operational duties would be fully handed over by 26 March and that I would come in on 28 March if needed. I remained available throughout the notice period. No attendance issue was raised at any time; in fact the clearance was signed off by every department.

Objection 7 — "We calculated according to our policy."

Response: The employment contract at Article 4 expressly provides that any condition conflicting with FDL 33/2021 is null and void. Internal policy cannot override the statutory calendar-day rule. If the internal policy arrived at 29 days total (first EOS), the policy needs to be re-run against the statute, not the other way round.

Objection 8 — "The second EOS already added a day. We've been reasonable."

Response: I genuinely appreciate the correction from 26 March to 27 March. That acknowledgement shows that the first document was wrong, and I thanked HR for the adjustment in my email of 7 April. But the clearance form signed the same morning as the first EOS already said 28 March and 30 days. The adjustment was partial. We are just one day away from closing this.

Objection 9 — "You've already opened a MOHRE complaint; you should withdraw before we settle."

Response: I opened the complaint only after the Article 53 deadline expired on 11 April and after the school transferred the file to their own legal department. I am willing to withdraw the complaint once the amended EOS is issued and payment lands in my bank account — that sequence protects both sides.

Objection 10 — "You are delaying the work permit cancellation."

Response: My resignation email from day one asked for a clean, immediate work permit cancellation before 28 March so my new employer could onboard me without delay. I have been the one asking for cancellation. The reason it has not yet processed is that the EOS document is not yet accurate — and I cannot sign a document that does not match the clearance the school itself issued. Fix the date and the cancellation can be processed today.

Objection 11 — "Signing the EOS is a formality; the waiver clauses won't actually be enforced."

Response: I would not ask the school to sign a document they believe overreaches, and I am in the same position. The EOS has non-solicitation, non-disparagement, confidentiality and indemnity clauses on top of the "full and final" language. I am happy to sign a correctly dated settlement — I am not willing to sign a waiver of statutory rights against an incorrect amount.

Objection 12 — "You are being difficult over a small amount."

Response: The amount in dispute is AED 666.67. I would much rather be done. The reason I am here is that the school's own signed clearance document says 28 March and 30 days — I am simply asking the school to honour what HR and the Principal already signed. If it were correct, I would have signed it two weeks ago.

Things not to say in response

  • Do not accuse HR or Athena of bad faith. Stick to the documents.
  • Do not bring up the verbal "no spring-break pay" conversation unless directly asked. If asked, confirm calmly.
  • Do not speculate about motive ("they're retaliating because…") — it opens the door to a fight you don't need.
  • Do not discuss salary negotiations, the new job, or anything not narrowly about the notice period.
  • Do not threaten escalation. The MOHRE ticket speaks for itself.

Ending the call well

"Thank you — I appreciate the mediator's time and I appreciate the school's willingness to come to the table. I would like to confirm in writing whatever we have agreed today so there are no misunderstandings, and I will keep the MOHRE record updated. Have a good day."
