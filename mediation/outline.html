Opening Statement & Speaking Outline

Script + structure. Read the bold lines verbatim if nerves take over; extemporise the rest.

Before you speak

  • Take one slow breath. You prepared. The paperwork is on your side.
  • Smile — even on the phone, it changes your voice.
  • Speak at three-quarters of your normal speed. Mediators need to take notes.
  • Do not interrupt. Let the school speak. Then answer calmly and factually.

1 · Greeting (≤ 30 seconds)

"Good [morning/afternoon], and thank you for taking the time to mediate this matter. My name is Brandon Walid Abdul-Baki. I was employed by Al Wahda Private School from 4 September 2025. I am here in good faith and I would genuinely like to resolve this amicably today."

2 · Framing the issue (≤ 60 seconds)

Keep it narrow. Don't broaden the fight.

"The dispute is a narrow one. I resigned during my probation period under Article 9(3) of Federal Decree-Law 33 of 2021. I gave a written 30-day notice, stating 28 March 2026 as my last day. The school's own signed clearance form — signed by HR and the Principal — confirms 28 March as my last day and 30 days of notice served. But the end-of-service document the school issued shows 27 March as my last day and pays me for only 29 days of notice. I am asking for the two documents to be consistent, and for the two missing days to be paid."

3 · The three undisputed facts (≤ 90 seconds)

Deliver these slowly, in order. Pause between each one.

  1. First — my resignation email of 26 February clearly stated 28 March as my last day, cited the specific articles, and asked for the full 30-day notice to be honoured. The school never objected in writing, never proposed a different date, and never responded at all during the entire 30-day notice period.
  2. Second — on 31 March the school's HR gave me a clearance form with the last date of employment written as 28 March 2026 and notice served as 30 days. The Principal signed it with the remark "No Balance Outstanding." Multiple department heads signed off. I signed it back the same morning.
  3. Third — two hours later on that same morning, HR emailed me an EOS that dated my last day as 26 March. That contradicted the clearance they themselves had just signed. I respectfully pointed that out, and the corrections have only partially been made.

4 · What I did (to show good faith)

This is where you show you are reasonable and have been throughout.

  • I called the MOHRE call centre on 800 84 to confirm the rule and relayed it to HR in writing.
  • I asked the school's HR to call MOHRE themselves if they doubted the calculation.
  • I followed up politely several times; the school replied that it was "full and final."
  • I waited out the 14-day Article 53 window — which ended on 11 April — before filing this complaint.

5 · The legal basis (≤ 60 seconds, only if asked)

ArticlePoint
Article 9(3), FDL 33/2021During probation, resigning to join another UAE employer requires a 30-day written notice. I gave exactly that.
Article 43, Labour LawNotice periods are calculated in calendar days, including rest days, holidays, and school breaks.
Article 53, Labour LawFinal settlement and work permit cancellation must be completed within 14 days of the contract end.
MOHRE call-centre guidanceA notice period commences the day after the resignation is submitted. 26 Feb → day 1 is 27 Feb → day 30 is 28 March.

6 · The ask (≤ 30 seconds)

"What I am asking for is simple: an EOS document amended to show 28 March 2026 as my last working day — matching the clearance form the school already signed — and payment of 28 days of March salary, which is AED 18,666.67. That is a difference of AED 666.67 from the document currently on the table. I am not asking for any penalty or extra; I am asking for my statutory notice period to be honoured in full so my work permit can be cancelled cleanly."

7 · Closing tone (≤ 20 seconds)

"I valued my time at the school and I would much prefer to close this amicably today. I am willing to listen to the school's position, and if there is anything I have not addressed that the mediator or the school's representatives need, I am happy to supplement the record."

Phrases to have ready

To the mediator, if something is off-record "I would be happy to supplement the record with that information if it helps."
To a leading question "I'd like to answer that carefully — could I ask you to repeat it, please?"
If pressured to agree to something on the spot "I am open to a resolution today. Could we put the proposed wording in writing so I can confirm I understand it correctly before I agree?"
If the tone becomes heated "I understand this is frustrating for everyone. I would like to stay focused on the documents we both signed."
To reset on the central fact "The signed clearance says 28 March and 30 days. That is the school's document — not mine."
